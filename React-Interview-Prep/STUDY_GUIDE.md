## 💡 Study Tips

### For Coding Challenges
1. **First attempt:** Solve without looking at solution
2. **Check solution:** Compare with provided solution
3. **Optimize:** Look for performance improvements
4. **Vary:** Solve similar problems from different angles
5. **Explain:** Describe your approach to someone

### For Questions & Answers
1. **Understand:** Don't just memorize, understand WHY
2. **Connect:** Link concepts together
3. **Examples:** Have real-world examples ready
4. **Trade-offs:** Know advantages and disadvantages
5. **Current knowledge:** Update based on React 18+ features

### For System Design
1. **Requirements:** Always clarify requirements first
2. **Trade-offs:** Discuss multiple approaches
3. **Scalability:** Think about 1M users, 100 developers
4. **Diagrams:** Draw architecture diagrams
5. **Details:** Go deep on interesting parts

## 🚀 During the Interview

### First 5 Minutes
- ✅ Clarify requirements
- ✅ Ask about constraints
- ✅ Understand scope (time limit, expectations)
- ✅ Ask about tech stack preference

### During Problem Solving
- ✅ Think out loud
- ✅ Explain your approach
- ✅ Discuss trade-offs
- ✅ Ask for feedback
- ✅ Admit when you don't know something

### Red Flags to Avoid
- ❌ Don't jump to coding immediately
- ❌ Don't ignore edge cases
- ❌ Don't be defensive about feedback
- ❌ Don't pretend to know something you don't
- ❌ Don't spend all time on one problem
